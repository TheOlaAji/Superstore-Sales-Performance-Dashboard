# Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard

## Interactive Power BI Sales & Profitability Analysis

This project uses the Sample Superstore dataset to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer orders, product performance, and regional trends.

The main deliverable is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to turn transactional retail data into clear business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Project Objective

The objective of this project was to build an interactive dashboard that allows business performance to be monitored through key sales, profit, order, quantity, and margin metrics.

The analysis focuses on:

- Sales performance
- Profitability
- Order volume
- Quantity sold
- Regional performance
- Product and sub-category performance
- Sales trends over time

---

## Dataset

The project uses the **Sample Superstore Dataset**, which contains transactional sales records from a retail business in the United States.

The dataset includes information relating to:

- Customers
- Orders
- Products
- Sales
- Profit
- Quantity
- Discounts
- Categories
- Sub-categories
- Regions
- Segments
- Order dates

## Dashboard Analysis

### Orders by Region

The dashboard compares order volume across the four regions:

- West
- East
- Central
- South

The **West** region recorded the highest number of orders, while the **South** recorded the lowest.

### Sales Trend Over Time

A time-series analysis was used to examine changes in sales performance across the available order dates.

The trend shows fluctuations in sales over time, with several noticeable peaks and periods of stronger performance.

### Profit by Sub-Category

Profitability was analyzed across product sub-categories to identify the products contributing most to overall profit.

**Copiers** generated the highest profit among the sub-categories shown, followed by **Phones** and **Accessories**.

### Quantity Sold by Category

The dashboard compares the quantity sold across product categories and their sub-categories.

**Binders** recorded the highest quantity sold among the products shown, while Paper and Furnishings also contributed significantly to total quantity sold.

---

## Key Insights

- The business generated approximately **$2.30M in sales** and **$286.40K in profit**.
- The overall profit margin was **12.47%**.
- The **West region** recorded the highest number of orders, followed by the East.
- The **South region** recorded the lowest order volume.
- Sales fluctuated over time, with several noticeable periods of stronger performance.
- **Binders** recorded the highest quantity sold among the products shown.
- **Copiers** generated the highest profit among the sub-categories shown, followed by Phones and Accessories.
- Interactive filters allow performance to be explored by **segment, region, category, and order date**.

---

## Recommendations

Based on the dashboard analysis:

- Focus on expanding sales in high-performing regions.
- Review underperforming sub-categories to identify opportunities to improve profitability.
- Optimize inventory planning for high-demand product categories.
- Monitor sales and profit trends regularly.
- Use interactive dashboard filters to investigate changes in business performance and support data-driven decisions.
